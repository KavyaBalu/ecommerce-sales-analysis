# Day 4 — E-Commerce Sales Analysis

## Work Completed
- Opened the e-commerce sales dataset in Excel.
- Identified the gross sales, discount amount, and net sales columns.
- Calculated net sales using the formula `=K2-L2`.
- Compared the calculated net sales with the original net sales column.
- Confirmed that all checked rows matched, with no mismatches.

## Key Learning
- Gross sales represent the sales amount before discounts.
- Net sales represent the sales amount after discounts.
- Excel formulas can validate calculations and help identify data inconsistencies.
